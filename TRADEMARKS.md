# vqro name and branding

The project's final name is **vqro** (lowercase; D27). The name/branding, official builds and maintained extension
repository identify this project. This is distinct from the copyright licence for original material.

Apache-2.0 section 6 does not grant a trademark licence. Modified distributions must not imply that they are an
official build or endorsed by the project. Factual attribution and the required licence/NOTICE retention remain
unchanged; this notice adds no copyright restrictions beyond Apache-2.0.

No registered-trademark status or public distribution/repository URL is claimed here. Historical decision and
research records retain the earlier name as written. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md), [LICENSE](LICENSE) and
[NOTICE](NOTICE) for the project's established Apache-2.0 and DCO terms.
